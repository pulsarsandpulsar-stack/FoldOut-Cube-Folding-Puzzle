Features

Built like the real test.
Explained like a tutor.

Every question is generated fresh, marked fairly and comes with a fold-up animation, so you learn why an answer is right, not only that it is.

Real test format

One net, four cubes, one right answer. The wrong options are near misses: mirror images, quarter turns, swapped faces and faces that can never be seen together.

Watch it fold

Scrub through a 3D animation of the net folding into the correct cube, then turning to match the answer on screen.

Hints that colour the joins

Stuck? A hint colours the edges that meet when folded, and the faces that end up opposite each other.

Timed presets

Quick, Standard, Sprint and Marathon sessions, or your own question count and pace. Practice mode or strict test conditions.

Find your weak spots

Every report breaks your results down by net and face style. Pro adds all-time accuracy, so you can see which folds keep tripping you up.

History and trends Pro

Every session saved on your device, with accuracy and speed charted over time.

How it works

Three steps. Every question.

The same routine examiners expect, practised until it is automatic.

01

Read the net

Six faces laid flat. Work out which edges meet and which faces end up opposite.

02

Pick the cube

Four cubes, each showing three faces. Only one can be made from the net.

03

Watch it fold

Check your answer, then play the fold to see exactly how the net closes into that cube.
